Chapter 1

Introduction to Accounting

Ch.1 · A

Book-keeping 與 Accounting 的分別

Book-keeping 簿記

refers to the process of recording transactions and keeping financial records.

＝ 只是「記低」交易。

Accounting 會計

provides financial information about an economic entity, which is useful for decision-making.

＝ 提供資料幫人做決定。Economic entity 例如 business company、NGO。

會計資料的用途：calculate profit / loss、calculate cash，再幫老闆做決定，例如 pay tax、buy shares、develop new market、find partners。

Ch.1 · B

Functions in accounting 會計的 4 個功能 MUST

次序要記住，第一步一定是 Recording。

Recording transactionsAll transactions are carefully recorded and become accounting data.
Classifying dataClassified according to their nature, e.g. Assets, Liabilities and Capital.
Summarizing dataPrepare financial statements, e.g. Income Statement and Statement of Financial Position.
Communicating informationReports to users such as owners and managers; useful for long-term plans e.g. opening a new plant.

Accounting is an information system that records, processes and communicates financial information about an economic entity in order to meet users' needs.

Ch.1 · C

Basic accounting concepts 兩個基本概念

1. Business entity concept 業務個體概念 MUST

  • A business is treated as an entity separate from its owner.（公司同老闆係兩個人）
  • Only transactions affecting the business should be recorded in the books of the business.
  • Accounting records should be kept separately for each firm.
Application 應用
✗Personal transactions of the owner should NOT be recorded.
✗Records should NOT be kept jointly for two or more firms.（合埋記）
✓Only when the owner brings additional capital into the firm, or makes drawings (takes money away), it is recorded.

例：Mr. Siu 從公司拿現金私用 → 要記（drawings）。他私人投資蝕錢、私人借錢、用自己錢買私人用品 → 不記。

2. Money measurement concept 貨幣計量概念

  • Only transactions capable of being expressed in monetary terms are recorded.
  • Qualitative items should NOT be recorded. 不能用錢計的都不記：
    • Good reputation of a company
    • The experience of staff（≠ salary 人工，人工要記）
    • The relationship with customers

例：Bank deposit 可以用錢表示 → 記；Staff loyalty、Business image、Customers' relationship → 不記。

Ch.1 · D

Accounting Equation 會計等式 MUST

Assets = Capital + Liabilities

變形都要識：Assets − Liabilities = Capital；Non-current assets + Current assets = Capital + Total liabilities。
✗ 錯的寫法：Total liabilities + Total assets = Capital。

1. Assets 資產

Assets are the productive resources of a business which are expected to benefit the business in the future.

Non-current assets 非流動資產

  • Long-term, benefit the business for more than 1 year
  • Physical substance
  • Material in amount
  • Buy for use in the operation of business, NOT for resale

例子（by durability，最耐用排先）

  1. Land and Buildings
  2. Premises 物業
  3. Plant and Machinery 工廠及機器
  4. Furniture and Fixtures
  5. Office Equipment
  6. Motor Vans (Motor Vehicles)

排序方法：Durability（more → less durable）、Purchase price（higher → lower）、Physical size（larger → smaller）、Importance in decision making（more → less important）

Current assets 流動資產

  • Short-term, to be sold within 1 year
  • More likely to be converted into cash

例子（by liquidity，最難變現金排先）

  1. Inventory 存貨（unsold goods）
  2. Accounts Receivable 應收帳款
    我們以 credit（賒數）賣貨或賣 non-current assets 給客，客欠我們錢
  3. Bank（cash at bank）
  4. Cash（cash in hand）

2. Liabilities 負債

Liabilities are debts of business owed to outsiders and not to the owners.

Non-current liabilities

Amounts due over 12 months（12 個月後先還）

  • Bank loan
  • Loan from XXX

Current liabilities

Amounts due within 12 months

  • Accounts payable 應付帳款
    我們以 credit 向 supplier 買貨或買 non-current assets，我們欠人錢

3. Capital 資本

Capital is the investment of the business to its owner since the owners have contributed cash or other assets to the business.

Double-entry System

Ch.2 · A

Double-entry 複式記帳 + 借貸規則 MUST

Transactions are entered in 2 accounts. One is on the debit side of one account and the credit side of another account. Each side should be the same amount.

Debit (Dr) = left 左邊　·　Credit (Cr) = right 右邊

Debit means increase in assets or decrease in capital or decrease in liabilities.

Credit means decrease in assets or increase in capital or increase in liabilities.

考試答題格：Double entry + Effect

每題左邊寫 Dr／Cr，右邊兩行各圈一個。Dr 那行只會是 ↑Assets／↓Liabilities／↓Capital；Cr 那行只會是 ↓Assets／↑Liabilities／↑Capital。

例：The company borrowed a loan of $1 000 from Barry Chow and deposited it into the bank.

Double entryEffect（圈一個）
Dr Bank 1 000( Assets ↑ / Liabilities ↓ / Capital ↓ )
Cr Loan from Barry Chow 1 000( Assets ↓ / Liabilities ↑ / Capital ↑ )

常見錯誤（講義上真的寫錯過）

  • 借錢存入銀行：Dr Bank / Cr Loan（不是 Dr Bank loan）。
  • 用 cheque 還銀行貸款：Dr Bank loan / Cr Bank（不是 Dr Bank）。
  • 老闆把 motor vans 放入公司：Dr Motor vans / Cr Capital（老闆投入的不只是錢，資產也算 capital）。
  • Returns outwards 是 Cr（Capital ↑），Returns inwards 是 Dr（Capital ↓）。
  • Bought machines in cash → 只是 Assets ↑ Assets ↓，Liabilities 不變。

Ch.2 (II)

Treatment of Goods 貨物的處理 MUST

Non-current assets

  • Long-term benefit which will be used for operation over 1 year
  • It will be classified as assets

Movement of goods 貨物

  • Goods are purchased or sold or returned for the prime purpose of resale
  • It will be classified as capital, because it will affect the profit or loss for the owner
Key termMeaningEffect on capitalDr / Cr
Purchases 購貨Goods bought by the firm for the prime purpose of resale−Dr
Sales 銷貨Goods sold by the firm which were bought with prime intention of resale+Cr
Returns outwards 購貨退出Goods returned to suppliers by the company+Cr
Returns inwards 銷貨退回Goods returned from customers to the company−Dr

邏輯：這四個都當 Capital 帳。Capital 帳 Dr 邊＝減（loss），Cr 邊＝加（profit）。所以 Capital 減 → Dr；Capital 加 → Cr。
